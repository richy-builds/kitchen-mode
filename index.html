Read the recipe from across the kitchen.

Kitchen Mode is a free button for your browser. Click it on a recipe page to see the steps in big type, one sentence at a time, with the ingredients each step needs and timers you start with a tap. Your screen stays on until you’re done.

Kitchen Mode

Set it up

  1. Show your bookmarks bar. Press ⌘ Shift B on a Mac, or Ctrl Shift B on Windows. This works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Safari and Firefox.
  2. Drag this button onto the bar.
    Kitchen Mode

  3. Open a recipe and click Kitchen Mode on the bar. Click it again, or press Esc, to close it.
Setting it up on a phone or tablet

Phones don’t have a bookmarks bar, so you save Kitchen Mode as a bookmark and paste its code in yourself. You only do this once.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap Copy the code.
  2. Bookmark this page: tap Share, then Add Bookmark. Name it Kitchen Mode.
  3. Open your bookmarks, tap Edit, then tap Kitchen Mode. Delete the address and paste the code in its place.
  4. On a recipe, open your bookmarks and tap Kitchen Mode.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap Copy the code.
  2. Bookmark this page with the star in the ⋮ menu.
  3. Open your bookmarks and edit the new one. Name it Kitchen Mode and paste the code into the URL box.
  4. On a recipe, type Kitchen Mode in the address bar and tap the bookmark that appears.

Which recipe sites work

Kitchen Mode doesn’t read the page you see. It reads the copy of the recipe that sites publish for Google, so ads, pop-ups and long introductions never get in the way.

A quick check: if a recipe shows a star rating or cooking time in Google results, Kitchen Mode will almost certainly work on it. Most food blogs work too, especially ones with a “Jump to recipe” button.

Won’t work

  • Recipes in videos, like YouTube, TikTok or Instagram
  • Recipes shared as photos, screenshots or PDFs
  • Sites that don’t publish their recipes for Google

If there’s no recipe to read, Kitchen Mode says so and leaves the page alone.

While you cook

Next line
Space or →, tap the right side, or swipe left
Back
←, tap the left side, or swipe right
Start a timer
Tap a highlighted time like ⏱︎ 5 mins, or press T
Tick off ingredients
Tap them on the first screen
Close
Esc, or click Kitchen Mode on the bar again

Your screen stays on while Kitchen Mode is open. A Bluetooth page-turner pedal or presentation clicker works as the next button too, so your hands never have to touch the screen.

Kitchen Mode is free and runs entirely in your browser. It reads the recipe on the page you’re on and doesn’t send anything anywhere.

Bookmarks don’t update themselves. To get a newer version, drag the button onto your bar again and delete the old one.
